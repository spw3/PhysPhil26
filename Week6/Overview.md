Quantum Mechanics

**Pre-Reading:**
1. Watch https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MzRCDLre1b4 and answer the following questions:
- What is a wavefunction? What information does it provide about a quantum system?
- What is superposition? How can a quantum system exist in a combination of possible states?
- What happens when we measure a quantum system?

2. Read through "The Problem with Quantum Mechanics" by Steven Weinberg, or the "Three Measurement Problems" by Tim Maudlin (https://scispace.com/pdf/three-measurement-problems-qwbn9am6h1.pdf) and summarise the different approaches we have when it comes to interpreting Quantum Mechanics.
